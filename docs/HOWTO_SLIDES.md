# Instruction slides (`howto`)

Hardcoded, voiced animation slides for custom games. Unlike a free `canvas` slide, the content is **code**, not data: the database only stores which topic to play, so no user-supplied HTML, text or media ever reaches the page.

## Data

- `slide_type = 'howto'` in `custom_game_questions`, column `slide_howto VARCHAR(32)` (migration `20261101000001_add_howto_slide_type.sql`, also added in `config/database.js`).
- `application/validators/HowToSlideValidator.js`: `slide_howto` is required for `howto` slides and must be one of `HOWTO_TOPICS` (closed list, currently the twelve topics `word_search`, `quiz`, `multiple_choice`, `order`, `matching`, `numeric_approximation`, `word_scramble`, `fill_blanks`, `survey`, `survey_multiple`, `hotspot` and `word_cloud`). Anything else is rejected by Joi (`validation.js`).
- The player sanitizer (`services/payload.sanitizer.js`) never copies `slide_howto`: players only receive `slide_type`.
- Scoring/timer treat `howto` as a slide (`QuestionScoringPolicy`, `GameUtils`); PDF export prints an info page (`pdf.service.js`).

## Client

- Core: `public/js/core/howto-core.js` (ES5; `tv.html` loads it too). A topic registers itself with `XiroHowTo.register(topic, { createStage, timeline })`; its texts live in `XiroHowToTexts[topic][lang]`. `mount(host, topic, { audio, language })` picks the language, builds the stage (1280x720 logical, scaled with `transform`), runs the timeline and plays the clips; `stop()` cancels timers and audio.
- Topic `quiz`: same three-file layout (`howto-quiz-ui.js`, `howto-quiz.js`, generated `howto-quiz-text.js`). Order of scenes: points at stake (a flip-style number that rolls for **random points**, then switches to **fixed points**; the creator's choice), the question with two to six colored answers (the demo shows four), tap (only the first counts), answer sent, an example of base + time bonus (fast / slow / wrong) and the result screen (green / red) with a streak bonus. The numbers on screen are illustrative (base 20, bonus up to 20, streak +50 %, the game defaults); the narration never says amounts, because the real base, bonus and streak depend on the game's configuration. Extra CSS: `.howto-tile*`, `.howto-points*`, `.howto-meter`, `.howto-result` in `css/howto.css` (no `gap`/CSS variables: the TV browser is old).
- Topic `word_search`: `howto-word-search-ui.js` (the phone + steps stage), `howto-word-search.js` (timeline: gestures start at a fraction of each clip's duration, so they follow the voice in every language), `howto-word-search-text.js` (**generated**: texts and clip durations in the 10 languages). Styles: `css/howto.css`.
- Shared frame for the newer topics: `howto-kit.js` (`XiroHowToKit`: phone with nickname and question, steps panel with caption, intro card, finger, «sent» screen, example score panel with rows `ok | bad | miss | perfect | info`, and the script wrapper that shifts the timeline by `INTRO_MS`). `quiz`, `word_search` and `multiple_choice` carry their own copy. Each of the other topics is `howto-<topic>-ui.js` + `howto-<topic>.js` + generated `howto-<topic>-text.js`, on that kit:
  - `multiple_choice` (six tiles ticked on and off, +10 per correct, −10 per wrong, +20 perfect bonus), `order` (four chips dragged top to bottom; streak up with all right, kept with half or more), `matching` (fixed left column, right column dragged; correct only with every pair), `numeric_approximation` (typed whole number; points = max × (1 − distance / margin), +20 for the exact number, 0 outside the margin: the example uses correct 300, margin 75, max 100), `word_scramble` (tap letters into boxes, extra letters, «Borrar», base + time bonus), `fill_blanks` (two blanks, one cell per letter, hints at −30 % each, a single time bonus, half of the blanks or more counts as a hit) and `survey` / `survey_multiple` (no points; results as percentages with the player's pick marked; the multiple one can add up to more than 100 %, and the button reads «Ninguna» with nothing ticked). `howto-survey-ui.js` and `howto-survey.js` hold both survey topics.
  - `hotspot` («Toca la imagen»): a fixed SVG picture (a house; «touch the chimney») drawn in `howto-hotspot-ui.js`, no photo and nothing from the database. Scenes: what to do, the question, a first wrong tap and a second one that moves the pin onto the chimney, send, the reveal (the area fades in over the picture while the example panel, shortened by `.howto-hs .howto-score` so the picture stays visible, lists inside = full points + extra, close = partial, far = 0), the streak (inside raises it, any points keep it, 0 breaks it) and the tip (tap the middle). It reuses the real `hs-pin--own` pin and `hs-area` polygon of `css/hotspot.css`. The numbers are never spoken.
  - `word_cloud` («Nube de palabras»): a phone with three word boxes (the demo says «hasta 3 palabras»), two of them typed (nobody has to fill them all), Send, and then the real cloud (`word-cloud-render.js`, same layout and fonts as the game) replaces the form and grows with other players' words; the most repeated word is the biggest; a repeated or banned word is not sent (notice «Ya enviaste esa palabra»). No points and no streak, so no example panel and nothing numeric is spoken. Files `howto-word-cloud-ui.js`, `howto-word-cloud.js`, generated `howto-word-cloud-text.js` (fields `words`, `cloud` as `[word, count]` pairs, `dup`); needs `word-cloud-layout.js` and `word-cloud-render.js` loaded before it (both pages already do) and `css/word-cloud.css` + `css/fonts.css`.
  - The example numbers on screen are illustrative: the narration of the scoring scenes never promises amounts.
- **Sound effects**: `builder.sfx(t, name)` in `howto-core.js` plays `/audio/howto/sfx/<name>.mp3` (tap, on, off, pop, send, ding, flourish, fall, fanfare) at volume 0.55, only when `audio` is true, so the TV stays silent. The eight clips were rendered with earshot from its shipped bank (`pro_tap/finger`, `toggle/on`, `toggle/off`, `ui_pop/blip`, `swipe/flick`, `pro_notify/ding_1930`, `mallet/flourish`, `ui_pop/fall`); the names are the whitelist `SFX_NAMES`. `fanfare` (2.75 s sampled brass, timpani and crash rendered offline from Apple's General MIDI bank, not in the earshot bank) is scheduled at t=0 by `mount` in `howto-core.js` (so every topic gets it, including quiz, multiple_choice and word_search, which do not use `Kit.script`), over the «<type> · Instructions» title, and ends before the voice starts at `INTRO_MS`. `createWebAudio.load()` also waits (max `WAKE_TIMEOUT_MS`) for the `AudioContext` to leave the suspended state, so a slow-waking output device (HDMI/Bluetooth) cannot delay the first sound past the clock. Tests that count played clips must filter by folder (voice vs `sfx/`).
- **Language**: the presenter uses the interface language (`XiroI18n.getLanguage()`); it restarts the animation on `xiro:language-changed`. The TV has no i18n (`i18n-stubs.js`), so it shows Spanish. A language without texts falls back to Spanish.
- **Look**: brand colors only (jungle background, plum phone, white cards with plum bottom edge, answer-tile hues), game voice (system sans 900), no emoji (mascot SVG instead). Each topic opens with a silent ~3 s intro card (`introName` + `introLabel` in its texts, e.g. "Sopa de letras / Instrucciones"); the timeline is shifted by `INTRO_MS` and the stage starts with class `howto-intro-on`.
- Presenter (`presenter/presenter-slide-howto.js`) mounts it in `#lobby-main` **with** narration. TV (`tv/tv-render-howto.js`) mounts the same animation **without** sound. Players (`player/player-slide-howto-ui.js`) and standalone mode only show «Mira la pantalla del presentador». Game music is muted on the slide (`presenter-music.js`, `hasAudio`).
- `XiroHowTo.stop()` runs on every slide change (`renderSlideOrQuestion`, TV `new-question`).
- Editor: `admin/modules/slides-howto.js`. The «Instrucciones» button opens a picker with one entry per question type; topics without animation yet (`ready: false`) are disabled with «Próximamente». The card edit modal changes the topic and footer.

## Audio playback

The presenter plays voice and effects with Web Audio (`createWebAudio` in `howto-core.js`): each mp3 is fetched once, decoded and played from memory, and everything is released on `destroy()`. The timeline starts only when all 17 sounds (8 clips + 9 effects) are in memory (6 s cap). Voice never overlaps, at most 4 effects sound at once, and audio that arrives more than 1.5 s late (blocked thread, background tab) is skipped instead of piling up. Without Web Audio it falls back to preloaded `<audio>` elements. Every voice clip ends with 450 ms of silence so output devices (HDMI, Bluetooth) do not clip the last syllable; when regenerating a clip, pad it and update its `dur`. The TTS cuts the end of the last word of a clip (e.g. «usan» lasted 0.16 s instead of 0.34 s), so clips are synthesized with a throwaway closing sentence (`TAIL` per language) and cut at the digital-silence gap that precedes it, with a 50 ms fade, then padded.

## Narration

- Clips `public/audio/howto/<topic>/<lang>/c0..c7.mp3` (one folder per topic, twelve in all) in es, ca, de, en, eu, fr, gl, ja, pt and zh, generated with VoiceStudio using the single cloned VoiceStudio voice "XiRo!" for every language (parameters in the `xiro-voz` skill, `.claude/skills/xiro-voz/voices.json`). After regenerating clips, bump `AUDIO_VERSION` in `howto-core.js` and rebuild the `dur` arrays. The 8 sentences per language are the `caps` of `howto-*-text.js` with the HTML tags stripped.
- Translations were written by Claude, not by a native speaker: have them reviewed (especially eu, gl, ca, ja, zh) before relying on them. Voices were approved by ear by the author; ca, gl and eu use the profile voice without `instruct`.
- **If a clip is regenerated, update its duration in `howto-<topic>-text.js`** (the timeline adapts to it).

## Adding a topic (one per question type)

1. Add the key (the `question_type`) to `HOWTO_TOPICS` (validator) and set `ready: true` in `_HOWTO_TOPICS` (`slides-howto.js`).
2. Create `howto-<topic>-ui.js` (stage, on `howto-kit.js`), `howto-<topic>.js` (timeline + `register`, with `h.sfx(...)` for the effects), `howto-<topic>-text.js` (texts in 10 languages with `dur`) and the clips in `audio/howto/<topic>/<lang>/`. When generating clips, reject any shorter than about the text length would need (a truncated clip is not caught by a noise check): in the new topics some Chinese and German clips came out at 0.1–0.6 s and had to be rephrased.
3. Load the four files in `presentador.html` and `tv.html`, and extend `__tests__/howto-slide.test.js` and `public/js/core/__tests__/howto-core.test.js`.
