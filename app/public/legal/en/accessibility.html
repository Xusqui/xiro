Accessibility statement

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Status

XiRo! aims to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. Conformance is partial and has not been assessed by an independent auditor.

What has been done

  • Skip links and keyboard navigation on the main pages.
  • Labels on form fields and descriptions on our own images.
  • Color contrast review in the panel and on public pages.
  • Fireworks are not shown if your device asks to reduce motion.

Known limitations

  • Games are played in real time with a timer, although the presenter can pause it.
  • The board and some animations are visual and have no full text alternative.
  • Images uploaded by question authors have no description of their own.
  • The TV version is available in Spanish only.
