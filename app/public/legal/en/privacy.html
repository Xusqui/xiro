If XiRo! is installed on a person's or organization's computer or server, that person or organization is responsible for the data in their installation.

What data, why and for how long

  • Panel accounts: username, email and hashed password, to manage the account and send confirmation and recovery emails. Basis: the relationship you accept when registering. Kept until the account is deleted.
  • Games: nickname, team, answers, correct answers, times and score, to run the game and review its results. Basis: legitimate interest in offering the game. Games are kept until the administrator deletes them. The presenter sees nicknames and answers; other players see nicknames and scores. Nicknames and final scores can be looked up with the session ID, and the administrator can share full results through a link.
  • Contact form: name, email and message, to reply to you. Sent by email and not stored in the database. Kept for as long as needed to handle your enquiry.
  • Security: IP address and technical connection data, to limit abuse and fix errors. Basis: legitimate interest. Application error logs are kept for 7 days, warning logs for 3 days and backups for 7 days. Other technical server logs are kept for as long as needed for maintenance.

Nicknames are free-form. You do not need to use your name or anything that identifies you. If you write something identifying, that is your decision and your responsibility.

Sharing with third parties

  • Hosting, network and email providers, which process data only to provide their service.
  • Licenses: each installation checks its license at auth.xiro.pro, which receives the version, a hardware-derived server identifier, its IP address and the key if there is one. Account licenses are validated there too.
  • AI generator: if an administrator uses it, the document or topic text is sent to the configured provider (Groq, Google Gemini or Ollama). Do not upload documents containing personal data.
  • Videos: YouTube, Vimeo and Dailymotion only receive your connection if the presenter taps the load-video button.
  • PowerPoint add-in: if you use it, a Microsoft library is loaded.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area.

Minors

You must be at least 14 years old to create an account. Anyone running games with minors is responsible for having the necessary authorizations.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability of your data by writing to (not configured). If you believe your request was not handled properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es). No automated decisions are made about you.
