Cookie policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

What is stored on your device

  • adminToken: the only cookie on this site. It keeps the admin panel session, is created only when you log in and lasts 12 hours.
  • Browser local storage: chosen language, data to reconnect you to a game (session, nickname, team and temporary key), presenter and remote control state, and panel session.

All of this is technical and necessary for the service you request.

No analytics or advertising

This site does not use analytics or advertising cookies or tools. That is why it shows no consent banner.

Third-party videos

YouTube, Vimeo and Dailymotion videos only load when the presenter taps the button. From then on, those services may use their own cookies under their own policies.

How to delete it

You can delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. If you do, you will be logged out and lose game reconnection.
